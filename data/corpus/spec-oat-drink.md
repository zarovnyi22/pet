# Oat Drink, Enzymatically Hydrolysed

**Supplier grade:** oat base (10% oats), rapeseed oil 1.5%, calcium carbonate 0.12%, sea salt 0.1%. UHT, ambient storage, use within 5 days of opening at 2–6 °C.

## Function

Plant-based replacement for cow's milk in bakery, pancakes and sweet fillings. During production oat starch is hydrolysed by enzymes into **maltose**, which gives natural sweetness (4.0 g sugars per 100 g, none added) and strong browning.

- **Hydration:** 1:1 replacement for milk by weight; viscosity is slightly higher (about 15 mPa·s vs 2 mPa·s for milk).
- **Browning:** maltose browns faster than lactose. Expect darker crusts; reduce oven temperature by 5–10 °C or bake time by 2–3 minutes for pale products.
- **Structure:** only 1.0 g protein, so it does not reinforce the crumb like milk proteins. Beta-glucans (0.4 g) improve moisture retention.

## Typical dosage

| Product | Dosage |
|---|---|
| Muffins, loaf cakes | 20–30% of batter, 1:1 for milk |
| Pancakes, waffles | 45–55% of batter |
| Custards | not recommended alone — weak set; combine 50/50 with soy drink or add 2% modified starch |

## Allergens

Oats are listed under **cereals containing gluten** (EU 1169/2011 Annex II). Standard oat drink must be labelled as containing oats/gluten. Only oat drink made from certified gluten-free oats (below 20 mg/kg gluten, EU 828/2014) can be used in gluten-free products. Free from milk, soy and nuts.

| Nutrient | Value |

| Energy | 46 kcal |

## Substitutes and pairings

- If the product must be gluten-free and certified GF oat drink is not available: use **soy drink** (spec-soy-drink) or rice drink.
- In high-protein formulas (custards, brioche) blend 50% oat drink with 50% soy drink to reach about 2.0 g protein.

## Cautions

- In trial-muffin-oat-drink a 1:1 swap for milk lowered specific volume by only 3% and the sensory score was equal to control (7.1 vs 7.0).
- Oat drink raises sugar per 100 g of batter slightly less than it seems: 4.0 g vs 4.7 g in milk, so sugar content actually drops.
- Barista versions contain dipotassium phosphate and 3% fat; do not use them in reduced-fat products without recalculation.
