# Allergen Management Policy for Reformulation

**Version 3.2 · Owner: Quality & R&D · Applies to all reformulation projects**

## 1. The 14 EU allergens

Under EU Regulation 1169/2011 Annex II these must be declared and highlighted in the ingredient list: **cereals containing gluten** (wheat, rye, barley, oats, spelt, kamut), **crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soybeans, milk** (including lactose), **tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites** (> 10 mg/kg as SO₂), **lupin, molluscs**.

## 2. Rules for substitutions

1. **No hidden allergen swaps.** A substitute must not add one of the 14 allergens unless the project brief allows it. Known cases:
   - milk → soy drink adds **soy** (spec-soy-drink);
   - milk → oat drink adds **oats/gluten** unless oats are certified gluten-free (spec-oat-drink);
   - egg → soy flour adds **soy**;
   - wheat flour → spelt or barley flour is **not** gluten-free;
   - butter → some plant blocks contain soy lecithin.
2. If an allergen is added, the change needs written sign-off from Quality and a label update **before** the first production run.
3. Every substitution must reference an internal specification (doc_id) or a supplier specification that lists allergens. A substitution without a documented allergen status is treated as **low confidence** and cannot go to production.
4. Check the full formula, not only the target ingredient. Example: a muffin with oat drink instead of milk still contains milk from butter (trial-muffin-oat-drink).

## 3. Preferred substitutes by goal

| Allergen to remove | First choice | Second choice | Adds allergen? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Milk (liquid) | oat drink 1:1 | soy drink 1:1 | oats/gluten; soy |
| Milk (butter) | plant block 80% fat 1:1 | rapeseed oil 0.8:1 | check lecithin source |
| Egg | aquafaba 45 g per egg + 0.5% replacer | flax gel 7 g + 37 g water | none |
| Gluten | GF-40 blend + xanthan/psyllium | — | none (dedicated line) |

## 4. Claims

- **"Gluten-free":** < 20 mg/kg gluten in the final product (EU 828/2014), dedicated line or validated cleaning, ELISA R5 test on every 5th lot.
- **"Milk-free" / "egg-free":** no ingredient containing the allergen; cleaning validated with an ELISA swab below 2.5 mg/kg.
- **"Vegan":** no animal-derived ingredients or processing aids; supplier declarations required for flavourings and lecithins. Vegan is not an allergen claim, so milk and egg cross-contact must be handled separately.

## 5. Precautionary labelling

"May contain" is used only after a documented risk assessment (shared line, shared equipment, airborne flour). It is never a replacement for cleaning. Wheat flour on a shared line requires "may contain gluten" on all products from that line, which makes a gluten-free claim impossible there.

## 6. Documentation

Each reformulation file must list: removed allergens, added allergens, the sources used (doc_id), and the updated allergen matrix.
