# Trial Report: Pancakes, Fully Vegan Version

**Trial ID:** TR-2026-024 · **Date:** 2026-04-22 · **Pilot kitchen, griddle 2**

## Objective

Make the frozen pancake fully vegan by replacing all three animal ingredients: whole milk, liquid egg and butter. Two milk replacements were compared: soy drink (spec-soy-drink) and oat drink (spec-oat-drink).

## Formulations (g)

| Ingredient | Control | Vegan-Soy | Vegan-Oat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wheat flour type 550 | 200 | 200 | 200 |
| Whole milk | 300 | — | — |
| Soy drink, unsweetened | — | 300 | — |
| Oat drink | — | — | 300 |
| Liquid whole egg | 100 | — | — |
| Ground flaxseed + water (flax gel) | — | 14 + 74 | 14 + 74 |
| Butter, melted | 30 | — | — |
| Rapeseed oil | — | 24 | 24 |
| Sucrose | 20 | 20 | 20 |
| Baking powder | 8 | 10 | 10 |
| Salt | 2 | 2 | 2 |

Egg (100 g = 2 eggs) was replaced with 2 "flax eggs" (7 g flax + 37 g water each, rested 10 min). Butter was replaced with rapeseed oil at 0.8 g per 1 g butter (ratio from the substitutes table in spec-butter; oil has no water, butter has 16%). Baking powder was increased by 2 g to make up for lost egg structure.

## Process

Batter rested 15 min. 50 g per pancake on a griddle at 190 °C, 75 s per side. Blast frozen at −35 °C, reheated 60 s in a toaster for evaluation.

## Results

| Measurement | Control | Vegan-Soy | Vegan-Oat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thickness (mm) | 7.0 | 6.4 | 5.6 |
| Diameter (mm) | 102 | 106 | 111 |
| Colour L* | 52 | 57 (paler) | 51 |
| Tears when folded | 0/10 | 1/10 | 4/10 |

Calculated batter nutrition per 100 g: control 201 kcal, 6.5 g protein, 5.5 g sugars; Vegan-Soy 180 kcal, 4.9 g protein, 3.6 g sugars.

## Sensory panel

12 panelists, 9-point hedonic scale.

| Attribute | Control | Vegan-Soy | Vegan-Oat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall liking | 7.2 | 6.9 | 6.6 |
| Texture | 7.3 | 6.8 | 6.2 |
| Flavour | 7.1 | 6.8 | 6.9 |

Vegan-Oat was "thinner and gummier". Vegan-Soy was "slightly pale", with no off-flavour noted.

## Conclusion

**Vegan-Soy approved.** It removes **milk and egg** from the allergen list but **adds soy**; the label and allergen matrix must be updated and the change signed off per guideline-allergen-policy. For better colour, raise the griddle to 200 °C or add 1% dextrose.

Vegan-Oat is the fallback for a soy-free product, but it adds oats (gluten); the flour already contains wheat, so the declared allergens are then only gluten.
