# Trial Report: Vanilla Muffins, −50% Added Sugar

**Trial ID:** TR-2026-027 · **Date:** 2026-05-06 · **Pilot bakery, line 2**

## Objective

Test whether added sugar in the vanilla muffin can be cut in half. Variant A used erythritol only (1:1 mass replacement); Variant B used the combined system from guideline-sugar-reduction.

## Formulations (g)

| Ingredient | Control | Test A | Test B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wheat flour type 550 | 250 | 250 | 250 |
| Sucrose | 170 | 85 | 85 |
| Erythritol (spec-erythritol) | — | 85 | 45 |
| Polydextrose (spec-polydextrose) | — | — | 40 |
| Steviol glycosides Reb A (spec-stevia) | — | — | 0.05 |
| Whole milk | 240 | 240 | 240 |
| Butter 82% | 100 | 100 | 100 |
| Liquid whole egg | 100 | 100 | 100 |
| Baking powder | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Salt | 2 | 2 | 2 |

In Test A erythritol is 9.7% of batter, above the 5–8% maximum for muffins in spec-erythritol; this was intentional, to find the failure point.

## Process

All-in-one mixing 3 min, 90 g per cup, 180 °C for 22 min. Baked yield ≈ 785 g per batch.

## Results

| Measurement | Control | Test A | Test B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specific volume (ml/g) | 2.45 | 2.08 (−15%) | 2.30 (−6%) |
| Sugars per 100 g baked | 23.4 g | 12.6 g | 12.6 g |
| Energy per 100 g baked | 329 kcal | 285 kcal | 290 kcal |
| Crust colour L* | 58.2 | 66.5 | 63.0 |
| Crystals on surface | none | visible on day 2 | none by day 5 |
| Water activity | 0.86 | 0.88 | 0.87 |

Total sugars drop by 46%, not 50%, because lactose from milk (11 g per batch) stays. Test A domed poorly: without sucrose raising the starch gelatinisation temperature, the batter set before it finished rising.

## Sensory panel

12 panelists, 9-point hedonic; sweetness on a JAR scale.

| Attribute | Control | Test A | Test B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall liking | 7.1 | 5.1 | 6.3 |
| Texture | 7.2 | 5.0 | 6.2 |
| Sweetness "just right" | 73% | 42% | 58% |

In Test A, 9 of 12 panelists noticed a cooling effect and 6 of 12 described the crumb as "dry and crumbly". Test B was "less sweet, slightly chewy".

## Conclusion

- **Test A rejected.** Erythritol alone cannot replace 50% of the sugar in a soft product.
- **Test B not approved:** overall liking 6.3 is below our release threshold of 6.5.
- Recommendation: limit muffins to **−30–35% sugar** with the erythritol + polydextrose + stevia system (as in trial-cookies-sugar-30). Retry −50% only with a flavour system (vanilla 0.3%) and baking at 170 °C for 26 min.
