# Liquid Whole Egg, Pasteurised

**Supplier grade:** pasteurised liquid whole hen's egg, 23–24% dry matter, stored at 0–4 °C, used within 7 days of opening. One medium shell egg equals about **50 g** of liquid whole egg (58% white, 31% yolk, 11% shell by shell-egg weight).

## Function

- **Structure (coagulation):** egg white proteins set at 62–65 °C, yolk at 65–70 °C. In sponge cakes egg is the main structure builder, not flour.
- **Foaming:** whites whipped with sugar increase in volume up to 6–8 times; this is the only leavening in genoise and sponge.
- **Emulsification:** yolk lecithin and lipoproteins stabilise fat and water in batters, custards and mayonnaise.
- **Binding:** holds cookies and muffins together and reduces crumbling.
- **Colour and flavour:** yolk carotenoids give a yellow crumb; proteins add browning.

## Typical dosage

| Product | Dosage | Main role |
|---|---|---|
| Sponge cake (genoise) | 40–50% of batter | foam + structure |
| Muffins | 10–15% of batter | binding, structure |
| Pancakes | 15–20% of batter | binding, tenderness |
| Cookies | 5–8% of dough | binding |
| Custards | 15–25% | setting |

## Allergens

Contains **egg** (EU 1169/2011 Annex II). Not suitable for vegan products.

## Substitutes (per 50 g whole egg)

| Substitute | Amount | Works in | Does not work in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aquafaba (spec-aquafaba) | 45 g | meringue, mousse, muffins, sponge (with support) | custards (does not set) |
| Flax gel: ground flaxseed + water | 7 g + 37 g, rested 10 min | cookies, muffins, pancakes | foams, sponge |
| Commercial replacer (potato protein + methylcellulose E461) | 8 g powder + 42 g water | sponge, cakes, muffins | — (check cost, about 3× egg) |
| Soy flour + water | 10 g + 40 g | pancakes, dense cakes | light products; adds **soy** allergen |

## Cautions

- Rule of thumb: if egg is more than 20% of the batter, egg is structural and no single substitute is enough. Combine aquafaba with a commercial replacer (see trial-sponge-aquafaba).
- Egg-free batters set later; extend bake time by 3–5 minutes or lower the temperature by 10 °C and bake longer.
- Removing egg lowers protein and fat. Always recalculate nutrition with calc_nutrition, not by estimate.
