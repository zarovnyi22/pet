# Aquafaba, Chickpea Cooking Liquid

**Supplier grade:** liquid from cooked chickpeas, standardised to 5–6% dry matter, pasteurised, no added salt. Chilled 0–4 °C, shelf life 10 days. Home-made aquafaba from canned chickpeas has variable dry matter (3–9%) and about 0.3 g salt per 100 g; it is not approved for production.

## Function

Aquafaba contains chickpea proteins (albumins, globulins) and saponins that form stable foams, similar to egg white.

- **Foaming:** whips to stiff peaks in 6–8 minutes at high speed; volume increases 5–7 times. Add 0.5 g cream of tartar per 100 g for stability.
- **Binding:** light binding in muffins and cookies.
- **No heat setting:** unlike egg, aquafaba proteins do **not** form a firm gel when heated. Products that depend on egg coagulation (custards, quiche, dense sponge) need an additional structure builder.

## Typical dosage

| Use | Replacement |
|---|---|
| 1 whole egg (50 g) | 45 g aquafaba |
| 1 egg white (30 g) | 30 g aquafaba |
| Meringue | 100 g aquafaba + 100–120 g sugar, dried at 90–100 °C for 90–120 min |
| Sponge cake | aquafaba foam + 0.5% potato protein or methylcellulose replacer on batter weight |

To concentrate thin batches: simmer until volume is reduced by 25–30% (about 8% dry matter), then cool below 10 °C before whipping.

## Allergens

Chickpea is **not** one of the 14 EU allergens. People allergic to other legumes (peanut, lupin, soy) may cross-react; mention "made from chickpeas" clearly in the ingredient list. Free from egg, milk and gluten.

## Substitutes

- **Commercial egg replacer** (potato protein + methylcellulose): better structure, higher cost.
- **Flax gel** (7 g ground flax + 37 g water per egg): binding only, no foam; best in cookies and pancakes.

## Cautions

- Foam collapses if any fat touches it; use clean bowls and fold fat in at the end.
- Foam stability: about 40 minutes at 20 °C, compared with more than 2 hours for egg white. Bake immediately after mixing.
- In trial-sponge-aquafaba, aquafaba alone gave −27% volume and a collapsed centre (score 5.4); aquafaba with 0.5% potato protein replacer reached −10% volume and a score of 6.8.
- Slight bean flavour appears above 30% of batter; mask with 0.2% vanilla or citrus zest.
