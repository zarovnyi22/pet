# Whole Milk 3.5% Fat

**Supplier grade:** pasteurised cow's milk, 3.5% fat, 3.3% protein. Stored at 2–6 °C, used within 72 h of opening.

## Function in bakery

- **Hydration:** main liquid in cakes, muffins, pancakes and enriched bread (87% water).
- **Browning:** lactose (4.7%) and milk proteins drive Maillard browning above 140 °C; crust colour is noticeably darker than with water.
- **Structure and tenderness:** casein and whey proteins strengthen the crumb; milk fat softens it and slows staling by about 1 day compared to water-based doughs.
- **Flavour:** creamy, slightly sweet dairy note.

## Typical dosage

| Product | Dosage |
|---|---|
| Muffins, cakes | 20–30% of batter weight |
| Pancakes | 45–55% of batter weight |
| Enriched bread (brioche, milk bread) | 30–60% of flour weight |
| Custards, fillings | 60–75% of formula |

## Allergens

Contains **milk** (EU Regulation 1169/2011, Annex II). Contains lactose. Not suitable for vegan products.

## Substitutes

| Substitute | Ratio | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oat drink (spec-oat-drink) | 1:1 by weight | muffins, cakes, pancakes | darker crust; oats = gluten allergen unless certified GF; see trial-muffin-oat-drink |
| Soy drink, unsweetened (spec-soy-drink) | 1:1 by weight | custards, bread, pancakes | closest protein (3.0 g); introduces **soy** allergen; see trial-vegan-pancakes |
| Rice drink | 1:1 by weight | allergen-free products | only 0.3 g protein → weaker crumb, add 1% pea protein on batter weight |
| Water + 3% milk powder | 1:1 | cost reduction | still contains milk, not a dairy-free option |

## Cautions

- Replacing milk alone does not make a product dairy-free: check butter, milk powder, whey and chocolate in the same formula.
- Plant drinks have 0–4 g less lactose-type sugar; if crust colour matters, expect L* values to shift by 2–5 units.
- For "vegan" or "milk-free" claims, follow guideline-allergen-policy (line cleaning, supplier declarations).
