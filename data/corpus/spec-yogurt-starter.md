# Yogurt Starter Cultures

Yogurt is fermented by *Streptococcus thermophilus* and *Lactobacillus delbrueckii* subsp. *bulgaricus*, often with added *L. acidophilus* or *Bifidobacterium*. The same bacteria can be supplied in forms that differ in allergens and vegan status.

## Forms of starter

| Form | Dosage | Grown on | Vegan | Milk allergen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bulk (mother) starter — fermented milk | 1–3% of formula | cow's milk | no | **yes** — it is milk |
| Freeze-dried DVS culture, dairy | 0.01–0.05% | dairy media; carrier often skimmed milk powder or lactose | no | yes, traces from carrier |
| Freeze-dried DVS culture, plant-based ("dairy-free", "vegan") | 0.01–0.05% | plant or synthetic media; carrier maltodextrin or dextrose | **yes** | no, if certified dairy-free |

A formula with 10 g starter per 1 kg (1%) almost always means a **bulk starter**, i.e. fermented milk. Removing milk from the base alone therefore does not remove the milk allergen.

## Nutrients per 100 g

| Nutrient | Bulk starter (fermented milk 2.5%) | Freeze-dried plant-based DVS |
|---|---|---|
| Energy | 55 kcal | 380 kcal |
| Protein | 3.0 g | 2.0 g |
| Fat | 2.5 g | 0.5 g |
| Carbohydrates | 4.0 g | 90 g (maltodextrin carrier) |
| of which sugars | 3.6 g | 5.0 g |

At 0.02% dosage the freeze-dried culture contributes below 0.1 kcal per 100 g of yogurt and can be ignored in nutrition calculations; the bulk starter at 1–3% cannot.

## Plant-based bases

- Dairy cultures need lactose. Soy and oat drinks contain none, so add 1–2% sucrose or dextrose as fermentation sugar, or use a plant-based culture selected for sucrose.
- Fermentation time on soy drink is similar to milk (4–6 h at 42 °C, target pH 4.5); on oat drink 6–8 h, and the gel is weaker (spec-oat-drink).

## Allergens and claims

| | Bulk starter (fermented milk 2.5%) | Freeze-dried plant-based DVS |
|---|---|---|
| Allergens | milk | none |
| Vegan | no | yes |

- For a "milk-free" or "vegan" claim, replace a bulk starter or dairy DVS culture with a **certified dairy-free culture** and keep the supplier's vegan declaration on file (guideline-allergen-policy).
- A dairy DVS culture in a plant product still requires a precautionary milk statement.

## Substitutes

| Original | Replacement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bulk starter 10 g | plant-based DVS culture 0.2 g + 9.8 g of the plant base | keeps total mass; nutrients ≈ plant base |
| Dairy DVS culture | plant-based DVS culture, same dosage | check the fermentation sugar |
