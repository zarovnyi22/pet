# Polydextrose (E1200)

**Supplier grade:** soluble glucose polymer, fibre content ≥ 90% (AOAC 2000.11), pH 2.5–7.0 in 10% solution, moisture ≤ 4%.

## Function

Polydextrose is a **bulking agent**: it replaces the mass and some of the texture effects of sugar, but has almost no sweetness.

- **Bulk and volume:** replaces removed sugar 1:1 by weight; keeps batter viscosity and yield.
- **Moisture retention:** hygroscopic; keeps cakes and muffins moist and lowers water activity by about 0.01–0.02 aw per 5% added.
- **Tenderness:** raises the starch gelatinisation temperature, like sucrose, which erythritol does not do.
- **Browning:** slight browning above 170 °C; better colour than erythritol.
- **Fibre claim:** adds dietary fibre; 3 g fibre per 100 g allows a "source of fibre" claim.

## Typical dosage

| Product | Dosage |
|---|---|
| Cookies | 3–8% of dough |
| Muffins, cakes | 4–10% of batter |
| Fillings | 5–15% |

Recommended use: replace **40–60% of the removed sugar mass** with polydextrose, the rest with erythritol (spec-erythritol), and fix sweetness with steviol glycosides (spec-stevia).

None of the 14 EU allergens. Produced from glucose (maize), sorbitol and citric acid.

## Tolerance and labelling

- Polydextrose is not a polyol, so the laxative warning is not required. It may still cause digestive discomfort above about **50 g per day**. Internal limit: 15 g per serving.
- Declared as "polydextrose" or "fibre (polydextrose)".

## Substitutes

- **Inulin (chicory fibre):** similar bulk and slight sweetness (0.1), but causes gas at a lower dose (above 10 g per serving) and browns strongly. Use at most 5% of product.
- **Soluble corn fibre / resistant dextrin:** very close in function, around 2 kcal/g.

## Cautions

- Batters with more than 10% polydextrose become sticky and cookies get chewy instead of crisp.
- Polydextrose absorbs moisture: store bags closed below 60% relative humidity or the powder forms lumps.
- In trial-muffin-sugar-50 variant B (erythritol 45 g + polydextrose 40 g) volume dropped only 6%, compared with 15% for erythritol alone.
