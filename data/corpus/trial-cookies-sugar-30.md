# Trial Report: Butter Cookies, −30% Sugar

**Trial ID:** TR-2026-017 · **Date:** 2026-03-19 · **Pilot bakery, line 3**

## Objective

Reduce sugar in the butter cookie by 30% so that a "reduced sugar" claim is possible (EU 1924/2006), without loss of volume or liking. Method follows guideline-sugar-reduction: the removed sugar mass is replaced 1:1 with bulking agents, and sweetness is balanced with stevia.

## Formulations (g)

| Ingredient | Control | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Wheat flour type 550 | 300 | 300 |
| Butter 82% | 200 | 200 |
| Sucrose | 150 | 102 |
| Erythritol (spec-erythritol) | — | 27 |
| Polydextrose (spec-polydextrose) | — | 21 |
| Steviol glycosides Reb A 97% (spec-stevia) | — | 0.06 |
| Liquid whole egg | 50 | 50 |
| Salt | 2 | 2 |
| **Total dough** | **702** | **702** |

Removed sucrose: 48 g (−32%), replaced by 27 g erythritol + 21 g polydextrose. Stevia was added as a 1:100 pre-blend with erythritol.

## Process

Butter and sweeteners creamed 4 min at 18 °C, egg added, flour mixed 1 min. Dough rested 30 min at 4 °C, rolled to 5 mm, cut to 50 mm rounds. Baked 170 °C for 12 min. Baking loss ≈ 8% (baked yield ≈ 646 g).

## Results

| Measurement | Control | Test | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sugars per 100 g baked | 23.8 g | 16.3 g | −31% |
| Energy per 100 g baked | 493 kcal | 467 kcal | −5% |
| Spread ratio (diameter/height) | 7.8 | 7.1 | −9% |
| Hardness, 3-point break (N) | 18.5 | 20.7 | +12% |
| Surface colour L* | 66.0 | 70.4 | paler |

Lower spread comes from less dissolved sugar. Paler colour comes from erythritol, which does not brown.

## Sensory panel

12 panelists, 9-point hedonic; sweetness on a 5-point just-about-right (JAR) scale.

| Attribute | Control | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Overall liking | 7.3 | 6.9 |
| Texture | 7.1 | 6.8 |
| Flavour | 7.4 | 7.0 |
| Sweetness "just right" | 75% | 68% |

Difference in overall liking is not significant (p = 0.21). 2 of 12 noted a slight cooling effect; nobody noted bitterness.

## Conclusion

**Approved** for the reduced-sugar range. The "reduced sugar" claim is valid: sugars are 31% lower and energy is not higher than the control. To improve colour, bake at 175 °C for 11 min. Do not raise erythritol above 15% of dough (cooling effect). Allergens unchanged: gluten, milk, egg.
