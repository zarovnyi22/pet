# Trial Report: Vanilla Muffins, Whole Milk → Oat Drink

**Trial ID:** TR-2026-011 · **Date:** 2026-02-12 · **Pilot bakery, line 2**

## Objective

Remove cow's milk as a liquid from the vanilla muffin and check whether oat drink (spec-oat-drink) can replace whole milk (spec-whole-milk) 1:1 without loss of volume or liking. First step toward a milk-free muffin.

## Formulations (g)

| Ingredient | Control | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Wheat flour type 550 | 250 | 250 |
| Sucrose | 170 | 170 |
| Whole milk 3.5% | 240 | — |
| Oat drink | — | 240 |
| Butter 82% | 100 | 100 |
| Liquid whole egg | 100 | 100 |
| Baking powder | 10 | 10 |
| Salt | 2 | 2 |
| **Total** | **872** | **872** |

## Process

All-in-one mixing, 3 min at medium speed. 90 g per cup, baked at 180 °C for 22 min in a deck oven. Cooled 30 min, packed in PE bags.

## Results

| Measurement | Control | Test | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specific volume (ml/g) | 2.45 | 2.38 | −3% |
| Crust colour L* | 58.2 | 54.1 | darker |
| Crumb moisture, day 1 (%) | 25.8 | 26.1 | +0.3 |
| Crumb moisture, day 3 (%) | 23.5 | 24.1 | +0.6 |
| Hardness day 3 (N) | 4.9 | 4.6 | −6% |

Darker crust is caused by maltose in the oat drink. Better moisture on day 3 matches the beta-glucan content.

## Sensory panel

12 trained panelists, 9-point hedonic scale, blind, randomised order.

| Attribute | Control | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Overall liking | 7.0 | 7.1 |
| Texture | 7.2 | 7.3 |
| Flavour | 7.1 | 6.8 |
| Appearance | 7.3 | 7.0 |

No significant differences (p > 0.05). 4 of 12 panelists noted a "light cereal" flavour; nobody rated it negatively.

## Conclusion

**Approved.** Oat drink replaces whole milk 1:1 by weight in muffins. To keep the original colour, reduce baking temperature to 175 °C for 23 min.

## Notes for further reformulation

- The product is **not yet milk-free**: butter still contains milk. Replace it with a plant-based block, 80% fat, 1:1 (see the substitutes table in spec-butter) in the next trial.
- Oat drink adds **oats (gluten)** to the allergen list. The muffin already contains wheat, so the label does not change, but this variant must not be used as a base for a gluten-free muffin.
- Sugar from liquid drops slightly (4.0 g vs 4.7 g per 100 g of liquid).
