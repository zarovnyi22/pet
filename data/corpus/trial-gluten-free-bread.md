# Trial Report: Sandwich Loaf, Wheat Flour → Gluten-Free Blend GF-40

**Trial ID:** TR-2026-021 · **Date:** 2026-04-08 · **Dedicated gluten-free room, line GF**

## Objective

Develop a gluten-free version of the white sandwich loaf using blend GF-40 (spec-gluten-free-blend) in place of wheat flour type 550 (spec-wheat-flour). Compare two hydrocolloid systems.

## Formulations (g)

| Ingredient | Control | Test A | Test B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wheat flour type 550 | 500 | — | — |
| GF-40 blend | — | 500 | 500 |
| Xanthan gum (E415) | — | 10 (2%) | 7.5 (1.5%) |
| Psyllium husk powder | — | — | 20 (4%) |
| Water | 310 (62%) | 400 (80%) | 425 (85%) |
| Dry yeast | 7 | 9 | 9 |
| Sucrose | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Salt | 9 | 9 | 9 |
| Rapeseed oil | 15 | 25 | 25 |

## Process

Control: kneaded 10 min, bulk proof 60 min, shaped, final proof 50 min at 35 °C, baked 220 °C for 30 min. Tests: mixed as a thick batter 5 min, rested 20 min, filled into tins, one proof 40 min at 35 °C, baked 200 °C for 50 min with steam for the first 5 min.

## Results

| Measurement | Control | Test A | Test B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specific volume (ml/g) | 4.2 | 2.4 | 3.1 |
| Crumb hardness day 1 (N) | 2.1 | 5.2 | 3.4 |
| Crumb hardness day 3 (N) | 3.0 | 9.6 | 6.8 |
| Crumb | fine, elastic | dense, gummy | even, slightly moist |
| Slice integrity | no crumbling | crumbles at 10 mm | holds at 10 mm |
| Gluten (ELISA R5) | — | < 5 mg/kg | < 5 mg/kg |

Psyllium forms a gel network that holds gas and allows higher hydration; xanthan alone gave a low volume and a gummy crumb.

## Sensory panel

12 panelists, 9-point hedonic scale, day 1.

| Attribute | Control | Test A | Test B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall liking | 7.4 | 4.8 | 6.2 |
| Texture | 7.5 | 4.1 | 6.0 |
| Flavour | 7.2 | 6.0 | 6.5 |

Test B was described as "moist, slightly dense, mild rice flavour". On day 3, Test B was described as "dry" by 7 of 12.

## Conclusion

- **Test A rejected** (volume −43%).
- **Test B approved** for the gluten-free range: 1.5% xanthan + 4% psyllium on flour weight, 85% hydration, 200 °C for 50 min.
- Shelf life reduced from **5 to 3 days** because of faster staling. Next trial: add 10% sorghum sourdough or an anti-staling enzyme.
- "Gluten-free" claim is possible (< 20 mg/kg) only if production follows guideline-allergen-policy: dedicated line, lot testing.
- Psyllium adds fibre: about +3 g per 100 g of bread.
