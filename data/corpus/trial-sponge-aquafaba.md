# Trial Report: Sponge Cake Base, Egg → Aquafaba

**Trial ID:** TR-2026-014 · **Date:** 2026-03-03 · **Pilot bakery, line 1**

## Objective

Develop an egg-free sponge base for the vegan layer cake. Egg is 50% of the batter, so it is the main structure builder (spec-whole-egg). Two variants tested: aquafaba alone, and aquafaba with a potato-protein egg replacer.

## Formulations (g)

| Ingredient | Control | Test A | Test B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liquid whole egg | 300 | — | — |
| Aquafaba, 6% dry matter | — | 270 | 270 |
| Cream of tartar | — | 1.4 | 1.4 |
| Egg replacer (potato protein + methylcellulose) | — | — | 3.0 |
| Rapeseed oil | — | 15 | 15 |
| Sucrose | 150 | 150 | 150 |
| Wheat flour type 550 | 150 | 150 | 150 |
| Baking powder | — | 4 | 4 |

Rapeseed oil (15 g) replaces some of the fat that egg yolk provided; baking powder compensates for weaker foam.

## Process

Control: whole eggs and sugar whipped 8 min to ribbon stage, flour folded in. Test: aquafaba and cream of tartar whipped 7 min to stiff peaks, sugar added in 3 parts, then flour (and replacer) sifted and folded, oil folded last. 500 g in a 22 cm tin. Control baked 175 °C for 30 min; tests 170 °C for 35 min.

## Results

| Measurement | Control | Test A | Test B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specific volume (ml/g) | 4.1 | 3.0 (−27%) | 3.7 (−10%) |
| Centre height (mm) | 52 | 34, collapsed centre | 47 |
| Crumb hardness day 1 (N) | 2.8 | 4.9 | 3.3 |
| Crumb structure | fine, even | dense layer at bottom | fine, slightly open |

Test A foam lost volume during the 10 min between mixing and baking; without heat-setting proteins the centre fell during cooling.

## Sensory panel

12 panelists, 9-point hedonic scale.

| Attribute | Control | Test A | Test B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall liking | 7.6 | 5.4 | 6.8 |
| Texture | 7.7 | 4.9 | 6.7 |
| Flavour | 7.4 | 6.2 | 6.9 |

Test A was described as "gummy" and "dense". 3 of 12 noted a light bean flavour in both test variants.

## Conclusion

- **Test A rejected.** Aquafaba alone is not enough when egg is above 20% of batter.
- **Test B approved** for the vegan layer cake: aquafaba 45 g per 50 g egg plus 0.5% egg replacer on batter weight. Bake at 170 °C for 35 min, and bake within 5 minutes of mixing.
- Add 0.2% vanilla to mask the bean note. Allergen status: removes egg; flour still contains gluten.
