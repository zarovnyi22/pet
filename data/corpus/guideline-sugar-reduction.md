# Sugar Reduction Without Loss of Volume

**Version 2.0 · Owner: R&D · Applies to cakes, muffins, cookies and pancakes**

## 1. Why volume drops

Sucrose is 18–35% of a typical sweet bakery formula (spec-sucrose). It gives bulk, traps air during creaming, and raises the starch gelatinisation temperature so the batter keeps rising for longer. If sugar is removed without replacing its **mass** and **functions**, specific volume drops by 10–25% and the crumb dries out.

## 2. Rules

1. **Replace the mass 1:1.** Every gram of sucrose removed is replaced by one gram of bulking agent.
2. **Use a three-part system:**
   - 40–60% of removed mass: **erythritol** (spec-erythritol), for sweetness 0.6–0.7 and bulk;
   - 40–60% of removed mass: **polydextrose** (spec-polydextrose), for bulk, moisture and tenderness;
   - **steviol glycosides** (spec-stevia) at 0.01–0.03% of product to close the sweetness gap.
3. **Step limits:**
   - up to **30%** reduction: allowed with the system above and one confirmation trial;
   - **30–40%**: full trial with sensory panel (n ≥ 12) required;
   - above **40%**: only as a research project; not approved for muffins or cakes (see trial-muffin-sugar-50).
4. **Ingredient limits:** erythritol ≤ 15% of product (≤ 8% in soft products, due to crystallisation and cooling); polydextrose ≤ 10%; stevia ≤ 0.05%.
5. **Process adjustments:**
   - erythritol does not brown: bake at +5 °C, or accept a paler colour (L* +3–6);
   - erythritol batters set earlier: for cakes, lower the temperature by 5–10 °C and extend baking by 2–4 min;
   - cookies spread about 10% less: flatten dough pieces to 5 mm.
6. **Recheck water activity** when sugar is reduced by more than 20%; target aw ≤ 0.88 for a 14-day ambient shelf life.
7. **Calculate nutrition with the calc_nutrition tool from ingredient specs**, never estimate by hand.

## 3. Claims (EU 1924/2006)

| Claim | Condition |
|---|---|
| Reduced sugar | ≥ 30% less sugars than similar products, and energy not higher |
| Low sugar | ≤ 5 g sugars per 100 g (solids) |
| No added sugars | no mono-/disaccharides or sweetening foods added; add "contains naturally occurring sugars" if present |

Polyols above 10% of the product require the warning "excessive consumption may produce laxative effects".

## 4. Reference results

| Product | Reduction | System | Volume | Overall liking | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Butter cookies | −31% sugars | erythritol + polydextrose + stevia | spread −9% | 6.9 vs 7.3 | approved (trial-cookies-sugar-30) |
| Muffins | −46% sugars | erythritol only | −15% | 5.1 vs 7.1 | rejected |
| Muffins | −46% sugars | erythritol + polydextrose + stevia | −6% | 6.3 vs 7.1 | not approved (< 6.5) |

## 5. Release threshold

A sugar-reduced product may be released only if overall liking is ≥ 6.5 and no more than 0.5 below the control. Products reformulated to remove an allergen (gluten-free, egg-free, vegan) are judged against the category benchmark instead, with a minimum overall liking of 6.0.
