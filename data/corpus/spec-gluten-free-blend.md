# Gluten-Free Flour Blend GF-40

**Composition:** rice flour 40%, tapioca starch 25%, potato starch 20%, sorghum flour 15%. No hydrocolloid included; add it separately per product. Gluten content < 20 mg/kg, verified by ELISA R5 on each lot (certificate of analysis required).

## Function

Direct replacement for wheat flour in gluten-free products.

- **Rice flour:** neutral flavour and main body; too much gives a gritty texture.
- **Tapioca starch:** elasticity and a chewy crumb, helps browning.
- **Potato starch:** softness and moisture; gelatinises at 58–65 °C.
- **Sorghum flour:** protein (10%) and a wheat-like flavour; improves colour.

There is no gluten network, so gas retention must come from **hydrocolloids**: xanthan gum (E415) or psyllium husk.

## Typical dosage (per 100 g blend)

| Product | Hydrocolloid | Extra water vs wheat |
|---|---|---|
| Cookies | 0.3–0.5 g xanthan | +0–5% |
| Muffins, cakes | 0.8–1.0 g xanthan | +10–15% |
| Pancakes | 0.5 g xanthan | +10% |
| Bread | 1.5 g xanthan + 4 g psyllium husk | +20–25% (dough hydration 80–90%) |

Rest batters for 20–30 minutes before baking so starches hydrate; this reduces grittiness.

## Allergens

Free from the 14 EU allergens when produced on a dedicated gluten-free line. Our supplier's line also processes buckwheat and millet (not allergens). Do not add standard oats, oat drink or wheat starch to products labelled gluten-free.

## Substitutes

- For higher protein and better colour, replace up to 15% of the blend with **buckwheat flour** or **chickpea flour** (chickpea adds a bean flavour).
- For softer cakes, increase potato starch to 30% and reduce rice flour to 30%.

## Cautions

- Baking: gluten-free bread needs longer baking at a lower temperature: 200 °C for 45–50 min instead of 220 °C for 30 min; core temperature must reach 98 °C.
- Staling: gluten-free crumb firms 2–3 times faster than wheat; in trial-gluten-free-bread hardness went from 3.4 N to 6.8 N in 3 days. Shelf life should be shortened from 5 to 3 days, or 2% of a sourdough/enzyme system added.
- "Gluten-free" claim (EU 828/2014) requires < 20 mg/kg in the final product; follow guideline-allergen-policy for line cleaning and testing.
