# Soy Drink, Unsweetened

**Supplier grade:** water, hulled soybeans 8%, calcium phosphate, stabiliser gellan gum (E418). No added sugar. UHT, ambient; after opening store at 2–6 °C for up to 4 days.

## Function

The closest plant-based match to cow's milk in protein content (3.0 g vs 3.3 g per 100 g), so it is the default milk replacement where structure matters.

- **Structure:** soy proteins coagulate at 70–80 °C and build a crumb similar to milk; recommended for custards, pancakes, milk bread and brioche.
- **Emulsification:** natural soy lecithin stabilises fat in batters; pancakes and crêpes release well from the pan.
- **Browning:** almost no reducing sugar (0.5 g), so crusts are **paler** than with milk. Add 1–2% dextrose or 3% oat drink on liquid weight if colour is required.
- **Flavour:** mild "beany" note that is masked at 0.2% vanilla or in chocolate and spiced products.

## Typical dosage

| Product | Dosage |
|---|---|
| Pancakes | 45–55% of batter, 1:1 for milk |
| Custard, pastry cream | 60–70%, 1:1 for milk, cook to 85 °C |
| Milk bread | 50–60% of flour weight |
| Muffins | 20–30% of batter |

## Allergens

Contains **soy** (EU 1169/2011 Annex II). Replacing milk with soy drink removes one allergen and **adds another**; the label must change and the switch requires sign-off per guideline-allergen-policy. Free from milk, gluten and nuts.

## Nutrients per 100 g

| Nutrient | Value |
|---|---|
| Energy | 33 kcal |
| Protein | 3.0 g |
| Fat | 1.8 g |
| Carbohydrates | 0.7 g |
| of which sugars | 0.5 g |
| Fibre | 0.6 g |

## Substitutes

- For soy-free products use **oat drink** (spec-oat-drink), but expect a weaker set in custards.
- Pea protein drink (2.0–2.5 g protein) is a soy-free alternative for pancakes; pea is not one of the 14 EU allergens.

## Cautions

- Curdling: soy drink separates below pH 4.6 or when boiled above 90 °C with added calcium; add lemon juice or fruit acids at the end, below 60 °C.
- In trial-vegan-pancakes soy drink scored higher (overall 6.9) than oat drink (6.6) as a milk replacement.
- Sweetened versions contain 2.5 g sugar per 100 g; use only the unsweetened grade in reduced-sugar formulas.
