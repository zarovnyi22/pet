# Sucrose, Fine Granulated White Sugar

**Supplier grade:** beet sugar, purity ≥ 99.7%, particle size 0.3–0.6 mm, moisture ≤ 0.06%.

## Function

Sugar does much more than sweeten. Before reducing it, check which of these functions the product depends on:

- **Sweetness:** reference sweetness 1.0.
- **Bulk and volume:** in cakes sugar is 25–35% of batter weight. Removing it without replacing the mass lowers volume and yield.
- **Aeration:** sugar crystals cut air cells into fat during creaming.
- **Tenderness:** sugar competes with starch and gluten for water. It raises the starch gelatinisation temperature from about 60 °C to 80–90 °C, so the batter rises for longer before it sets.
- **Colour:** caramelisation starts at about 160 °C; with proteins, Maillard browning starts from 140 °C.
- **Water activity and shelf life:** lowers aw; a cake with 30% sugar has aw about 0.85, which slows mould growth.
- **Spread:** in cookies, dissolved sugar makes the dough more fluid and increases spread.

## Typical dosage

| Product | Dosage |
|---|---|
| Sponge and butter cakes | 25–35% of batter |
| Muffins | 18–25% of batter |
| Cookies | 20–30% of dough |
| Pancakes | 2–6% of batter |
| Yeast bread | 0–8% of flour weight |

## Substitutes for sugar reduction

| Replacement system | Replaces | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Erythritol (spec-erythritol) | sweetness 0.6–0.7, bulk 1:1 | max 15% of product; cooling taste; no browning |
| Polydextrose (spec-polydextrose) | bulk only | no sweetness; use with a high-intensity sweetener |
| Steviol glycosides (spec-stevia) | sweetness only, 200–300× | 0.02–0.05% of product; bitter above 0.05% |
| Erythritol + polydextrose + stevia | all three | preferred system; 30% reduction approved in trial-cookies-sugar-30 |

Allulose is **not authorised** in the EU as a novel food; do not use it in EU formulations.

## Cautions

- Follow guideline-sugar-reduction: up to 30% reduction by replacing removed mass 1:1 with bulking agents; above 30% needs a full trial.
- A 50% reduction in muffins failed in trial-muffin-sugar-50 (volume −15%).
- Less sugar raises water activity; recheck shelf life when sugar drops by more than 20%.
